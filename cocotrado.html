The problem

What today looks like, and what changes

Four things every team told us. On the left, the way it is done now. On the right, what CocoTrado does about it.

TODAYThe balance lives in a spreadsheet

One sheet, one person who understands it, and a number the client has to take on trust.

WITH COCOTRADOOne ledger per client per currency

Opening, received, paid, charges, fees, adjustments. The running balance is right at every line and never combined across currencies.

TODAYCurrencies get mixed

AED in, USD out, a rate remembered from that day. The statement adds things that should never be added.

WITH COCOTRADOBoth sides of every payment

What left the client’s balance, what the supplier received, and the rate between them, stored on the row forever.

TODAYOne person can pay

The same person prepares and releases a supplier payment. The bank and the auditor both notice.

WITH COCOTRADOMaker and checker

Finance prepares, a different person approves. No role holds both, and the database refuses anything else.

TODAYStatements are built by hand

Every month, an evening of copy and paste, and a figure nobody can reproduce later.

WITH COCOTRADOA statement in one click

A three-sheet Excel with live formulas and a PDF, per client, per period, archived so it reproduces exactly.

Questions

What people ask before they start

Is this accounting software?+
No. It is the custody ledger for money you hold for clients. Your own books stay in your accounting tool.
Can my client log in?+
No, by design. Clients receive the statement, in Excel and PDF, and never see the application.
We don’t trade coffee. Does it matter?+
No. Nothing is hardcoded: currencies, suppliers, fee rules, document types and deal numbering are configured by your Admin.
Can I correct a mistake?+
Yes: by reversal, or by an adjustment with a reason that appears on the statement. Nothing is ever deleted.
How do we start?+
Run one month alongside your existing spreadsheet and reconcile the closing balance per client per currency. Then switch.
