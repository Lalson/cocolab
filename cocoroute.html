The problem

What today looks like, and what changes

Four things every team told us. On the left, the way it is done now. On the right, what CocoRoute does about it.

TODAYEnquiries live in WhatsApp and inboxes

One comes by WhatsApp, one by email, one by phone. A week later nobody can find the rate that was quoted.

WITH COCOROUTEOne pipeline

Every enquiry becomes a card, from new to won, whichever way it came in. The history stays on the card.

TODAYQuotes are retyped every time

Each quote starts from a blank sheet. Same lanes, same surcharges, typed again, with a different mistake each time.

WITH COCOROUTEQuotes in minutes

Pick the lane and the charges you saved last time. Send a clean PDF with your logo on it.

TODAYFollow-ups depend on memory

The customer said “call me Thursday”. Thursday came and went. The shipment went to the forwarder who called.

WITH COCOROUTEReminders that chase

A follow-up date on every enquiry. Today’s calls sit in one list until each one is done.

TODAYThe owner can’t see the pipeline

“How many quotes are open this week?” is answered by asking everyone in the office.

WITH COCOROUTEA live overview

Open quotes, what closed this month, and who has what, on one screen for the manager.

Questions

What people ask before they start

Is it only for sea freight?+
No. Sea, air and land; the mode is a field on the enquiry, and a quote can carry more than one leg.
Can I import my customer list?+
Yes, from a spreadsheet. Companies, contacts and notes come in; duplicates are shown before they are saved.
Does it replace my operations system?+
No. CocoRoute runs the sales side: enquiries, quotes, customers and follow-ups. Operations and accounts keep their tools.
Who sees what?+
Each salesperson sees their own customers and enquiries. The manager sees everyone. You decide who else gets in.
How is it priced?+
Per user, per month. Book a demo and we will quote for your team size.
