The problem

What today looks like, and what changes

Four things every team told us. On the left, the way it is done now. On the right, what CocoMio does about it.

TODAYAttendance on paper and WhatsApp

Who came in, who was late, who is on leave: a register, a group chat and memory. Month-end takes days.

WITH COCOMIOA punch clock with a geofence

In and out from the phone, only at the office. The month closes with one click and exports to payroll.

TODAYA visa expires and nobody noticed

Passports, visas, Emirates IDs and contracts sit in a drawer. The reminder is the fine.

WITH COCOMIODocuments that warn you

Every document with its date and its file. Reminders reach the employee and HR at 90, 60 and 30 days.

TODAYTraining is scattered

A procedure in a PDF, a voice note, a day of shadowing. Nobody can say who actually knows the process.

WITH COCOMIOAn Academy from your own documents

Lessons and question banks drafted from your procedures, reviewed by a person, then assigned by role.

TODAYPromotion feels arbitrary

Employees don’t know what to learn or pass to reach the next level, so the good ones leave.

WITH COCOMIOA career ladder everyone can read

Where I am, what I must learn, which exams, which level is next, what salary band, and how close I am.

Questions

What people ask before they start

Can we use it outside the UAE?+
Yes. Each office carries its country, currency and working week, and end-of-service rules are set per country.
Who writes the courses?+
Your own documents are the source. Lessons and questions can be drafted from them, but a named person reviews and approves everything before any employee sees it.
Can employees see each other’s salaries?+
No. Money leaves the server only when asked for by name, and every reveal of somebody else’s pay is logged.
Does it run payroll?+
It closes the month and exports the sheet your payroll needs. Payroll itself stays in your existing tool.
What if we have 200 people?+
CocoMio is built for companies under a hundred. If you are larger, talk to us before deciding.
