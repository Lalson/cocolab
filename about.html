About CocoLabs

Software for work that has to be right.

CocoLabs OÜ is a software company registered in Tallinn, Estonia, in the European Union. We make three products, each for one kind of work: the freight business, people's attendance and careers, and clients' money.

Why we started

Why CocoLabs exists

CocoLabs started in logistics. Every shipment brings a stream of emails, WhatsApp messages and booking confirmations, and the one detail you need is always buried somewhere in them. Keeping on top of it by hand was hard every single day, so we decided to build a better way. That became CocoRoute.

We saw the same with staff. Attendance, leave, payroll, performance and training all matter, yet they sat in different registers, chats and spreadsheets. So we made one system for all of it, with its own academy where staff learn and move up. That became CocoMio.

CocoTrado does the same for buying agents who handle their clients' money. Each product does one job, and all of them follow the same rules: history is kept, a named person decides, and each company's data stays its own.

How we build

Four rules every product follows

The same rules hold whatever job the product does.

01

Records you can trust

History is added to, never rewritten. Corrections stay visible, so a figure you sent last year can still be explained.

02

A person approves

Software suggests; a named person decides. Every decision leaves a record.

03

Your data stays yours

Each company's data is kept apart from every other company's. Nobody else's customers, people or money are in it.

04

Small, focused products

Each product does one job, with its own website. They share a team, not a login.
